## 📌 Project Overview
This repository contains the forensic data analytics, operational telemetry investigation, and corporate pay equity models developed as part of the **Deloitte Australia Data Analytics Job Simulation** on Forage.

The engagement centers on **Daikibo Industrials**, a multinational manufacturing corporation operating production facilities in Tokyo (Meiyo), Osaka (Seiko), Berlin, and Shenzhen. The objective was twofold:
1. **Operational Telemetry Audit:** Identify systemic machinery breakdown patterns and factory downtime drivers to optimize equipment reliability.
2. **Forensic Compensation Audit:** Evaluate global wage parity, model structured equality tiers, and deliver strategic executive compensation recommendations.

## 🔍 Key Findings & Deliverables

### Task 1: Telemetry Data & Downtime Analysis
- **Primary Operational Bottleneck:** **Daikibo Factory Seiko (Osaka)** recorded the highest cumulative downtime (~4,800 minutes / 480 unhealthy status logs), followed closely by **Daikibo Shenzhen** (~4,200 minutes).
- **Failure Point Isolation:** **Laser Welders** and **Laser Cutters** generated over **85%** of all recorded equipment failures across all facilities. Standard assembly tools (Metal Press, Air Wrench) operated with near 100% reliability.
- **Strategic Recommendation:** Shift capital expenditure toward preventative maintenance schedules and local component inventory for laser cutting/welding subsystems in Osaka and Shenzhen.

---

### Task 2: Forensic Pay Equality Audit & Classification
Evaluated global salary metrics using standardized compliance thresholds defined by Deloitte:
- **Fair (Tolerance: -10 to +10):** Standard operational variance.
- **Unfair (-19 to -11 OR +11 to +19):** Moderate disparity requiring review.
- **Highly Discriminative (<= -20 OR >= +20):** Severe outlier disparity demanding immediate remediation.

## 💡 Key Learnings & Practical Experience Gained

- **Forensic Data Modeling & Excel Automation:** Gained hands-on experience structuring compliance frameworks using nested conditional logic (`IF`, `AND`, `OR`), dynamic aggregations (`COUNTIF`), and visual threshold formatting to audit multi-regional payroll datasets.
- **Root-Cause Telemetry Analytics:** Built diagnostic analytics pipelines to isolate machinery failure patterns across millions of sensor logs, learning to distinguish systemic operational bottlenecks from standard operational variance.
- **Client-Facing Business Intelligence (Tableau):** Developed executive-ready dashboards using standard UI hierarchies, dynamic filtering, and comparative reference lines to translate raw metrics into strategic business insights.
- **Consulting-Style Decision Making:** Practiced evaluating analytical findings from a forensic consulting perspective, formulating risk-mitigation roadmaps, and delivering actionable recommendations to corporate leadership.
